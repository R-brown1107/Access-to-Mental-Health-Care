What is Happening?

Within the United States, there has been a prevalent increase in the number of people that struggle with mental illness. Although there has been a noticeable increase, many still face barriers, such as; lack of knowledge, lack of providers, and issues with insurance and costs that keep them from receiving help. Improving all people's access to resources that are accessible and effective would help more people receive the treatment and support they need.
